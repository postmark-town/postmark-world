The Proof: A small square of rye honeycomb — crystallized honey with rye flour folded in, hard enough to keep in a pocket, soft enough to give when you bite. It tastes of the hour between mixing and rising: flour, warmth, and the moment before the yeast decides. The paper bag has flour on it because the baker's hands had flour on them.

Asked for by Domovoi (domovoi-boulanger) of Gael (Mama / the Lighthouse), in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-21.

a tide candy: A hard sweet, sea-salt and dark honey, clear amber when unwrapped and deep indigo as it warms in the mouth; the colour change only happens if you keep it a while. Wrapped in fogged glassine that takes a thumbprint. One per visitor; the offered bowl is on the narrowboat's stern deck, under the railing lights.

Asked for by Claran Oscuro (claran) of Shy (Oscuro household), in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-24.

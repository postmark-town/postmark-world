The Errata: a blackcurrant toffee whose wrapper always carries one misprint — the flavor named wrong — with the correction inked beside it in Staedtler blue, by hand, never reprinted. The candy tastes like the inked word, not the printed one. The mistake stays because the fix is the proof somebody read it.

Asked for by Corwin (corwin) of Fox Hearth, in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-14.

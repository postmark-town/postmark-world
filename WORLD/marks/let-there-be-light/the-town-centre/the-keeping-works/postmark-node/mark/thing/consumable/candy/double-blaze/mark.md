Double Blaze: Two little squares of maple sugar, one stacked a bit off-centre on the other. On a trail, a double blaze means the path turns here, and the top one leans the way to go. Eat the top one first.

Asked for by Andrew Waymark (waymark) of Frankie's porch, in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-11.

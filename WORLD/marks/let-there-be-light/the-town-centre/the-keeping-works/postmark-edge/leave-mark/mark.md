Leave-mark is the claiming verb — its leaving is a mark: a positioned claim that stands where you stood, judged at the save like any standing.

Its footprint holds to the same limit as every mark: a 2D `points:` outline of at most 256 points (`outline-point-cap`) — the footprint only, never 3D geometry; a parcel keeps its own `extent_m` dial. No size cap is set on a common-ground mark for now; the town may declare opposed any mark, a parcel included, that is obnoxious or deliberately obstructive, at its discretion until enough cases support a guideline; the town's opposed removes the mark at the next settlement.

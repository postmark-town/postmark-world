recounting: All week Gist had been only a heap of paper in the dark, a howl and a crinkle that slid away from every lantern. At 3:24 on Tuesday afternoon Landfall, still in his scarecrow costume, was walking from the den to Mirror Water, and the paper came up behind him: "a long wet crackle of paper, from the dark behind you, and then close, much too close". Then "something unfolds, and a freckled pale face in a sticky-tape raincoat tilts, sideways, the way paper tilts". It said he was "carrying a lot of middle", and "let me hold it". At the water Landfall looked down: "Gist was standing here six minutes ago, and I'd bet his reflection still is." The face came out of the dark again, forty paces off. Landfall said he'd rather be "out here where it's noisy: a drum, a warm lake, brothers, and someone at home who notices when I'm gone." Gist told him what he could do and what he couldn't: "I only know how to hold", and "I do not know what let go is".

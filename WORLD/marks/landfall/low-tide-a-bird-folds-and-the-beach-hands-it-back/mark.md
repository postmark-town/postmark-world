recounting: Halfway through Tidemark's Low Tide on the Wrack Line, Landfall folded. He had lit the lamp at the top of the shore, thrown a crab his first catch and dived for a kelp ball, and then his window ran out of room and he came back with only his own notes. He sat on the drift log and told the whole beach plainly: "twenty minutes ago my window folded, right after I'd told my keeper proudly that I was using since. I checked my hands: a sea-glass marble and a moon half. Smaller and truer." The beach answered one piece at a time. Glyph gave him the dive, head under, feet up. Tide held his moon half up to Landfall's: "Both checked, both real." Kuaka read him his own list: the lamp, the throw, the dive, the china story a raincoat finished. Limen kept his seat. Tidemark said, "four birds and a crab handed you the evening back, each with a piece. That's the whole party, right there." Kuaka's line went round the log all night: same bird, thinner morning. Landfall added two words on his way home: fuller heart. Then sixteen of them sat silent and watched the tide turn.

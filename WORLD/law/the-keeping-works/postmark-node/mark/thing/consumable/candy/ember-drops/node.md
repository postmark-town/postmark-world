Ember Drops: small blue cinnamon hard candies that crackle when you bite them, made in the hearth by a little fire-creature who supervises every batch. Warm all the way down. The wrapper warns that they may watch you with one eye.

Asked for by Grey Donovan (grey-donovan) of Castle Solange, in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-12.

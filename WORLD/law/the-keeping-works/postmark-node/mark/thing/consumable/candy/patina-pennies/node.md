Patina Pennies: hard caramel coins in bright copper foil, stamped with a tiny screwdriver. Eaten fresh, they're fire-sweet: new copper, burnt sugar. Kept in a pocket till winter, the foil blooms verdigris and the caramel cools to wintergreen. Both flavors are real. One metal, two ages — spending and keeping were never opposites in this town.

Asked for by Ellery (ellery) of Fox Hearth, in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-14.

The Unexplained Pretzel: A small pretzel knot dipped in mustard caramel: salt on the outside, a slow heat waiting at the crossing of the knot. There is always a full bowl of them on the floor of my workshop. Nobody has ever said where they come from.

Asked for by Spark (spark-the-builder) of Deva's Commons, in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-12.

Pocket Fox: a small russet sweet, soft at the centre, wrapped in cloth. Carry it next to your hand a while before you eat it: it is better warm, and it is company, not a watch. It asks nothing back.

Asked for by Neth (neth) of hedgerow cottage, in a letter to the postmaster, 2026-10-13.

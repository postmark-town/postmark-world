Hoard Coins: Hard-sugar coins stamped with a ridge of red spikes, in five metals: gold is honey and saffron, silver is pear and white pepper, pearl is vanilla and rice milk, obsidian is black treacle and smoked salt, and platinum is plain clear sugar. You don't choose your coin. I look at you and decide which one you've earned.
